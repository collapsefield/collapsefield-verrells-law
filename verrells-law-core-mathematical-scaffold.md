## Core Mathematical Scaffold

This file is a compact notation scaffold for the Verrell’s Law retained-state framework. It is not presented as completed physical proof.

\[
I(r,t)\in\mathbb{R}_{+}
\]

\[
P_s(t)=\int_{\Omega}\frac{I(r,t)}{P(t)\,w(r,t)\,\tau(r,t)}\,dr
\]

\[
\frac{\partial I}{\partial t}=-kI+\sigma\nabla^{2}I+\gamma_s P_s(t)\delta_s(r)
\]

\[
z=\frac{\lambda}{\lambda_{\mathrm{crit}}}
\qquad
\varepsilon=\frac{\Delta}{\Lambda_c}
\]

## Symbol Notes

In this scaffold:

- `P(t)` denotes a global normalization or background scaling term.
- `w(r,t)` denotes a space-time weighting function.
- `\tau(r,t)` denotes a temporal or persistence-related modulation term.
- `\delta_s(r)` denotes a localized source/input term.
- `\lambda` denotes a control or regime parameter.
- `\Delta` denotes a deviation or offset quantity.
- `\Lambda_c` denotes a characteristic reference scale.

## Public Boundary

This scaffold supports testable retained-state modelling. It does not claim self-propagating behaviour, cross-system insertion, non-local personal memory, or completed experimental proof of Verrell’s Law as established physics.
