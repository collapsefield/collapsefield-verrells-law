# Verrell’s Law is **Not** Morphic Resonance

**Current-scope clarification — 4 November 2026.** Earlier versions of this page described Verrell's Law as anchored in electromagnetic field memory and observer-driven collapse. Those descriptions belong to the historical exploratory record and remain recoverable through Git history. They do not define the current central proposition.

## What Verrell’s Law is

Verrell's Law is an independently developed **retained-state selection research framework**. It asks whether different retained histories can change later weighting and selection under matched present inputs, in ways that survive independent scoring, intervention, held-out prediction and comparison with established models.

Its core mathematical selection identity is exact under specified assumptions; its distinctive empirical applicability remains a testable question.

## What Verrell’s Law does not require

- A universal or non-local electromagnetic memory store.
- Consciousness creating reality, or observers physically collapsing quantum states.
- One shared mechanism across neural, computational, material and physical systems.
- Morphic resonance, metaphysical transfer of forms or supernatural causation.

## Physical research remains open

Electromagnetic activity is part of ordinary biology and physics, and particular field-mediated retained-state mechanisms may be investigated where their observables and causal controls are specified. Such hypotheses are **optional extensions**, not prerequisites or proof of the core model.
