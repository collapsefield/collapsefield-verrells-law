## 1. Retained-State Selection

**Retained-State Selection** is the current technical process/category language:

> **Retained-State Selection is the controlled study or process by which information preserved from prior states is permitted to influence selection among presently available candidate outcomes.**

The general ideas of retained state, memory, path dependence, hysteresis, stateful computation, candidate scoring and policy engines are not claimed as original.

The current focus is whether retained history contributes measurable, candidate-relative influence over later selection and whether that influence can be isolated, compared, ablated/intervened upon, predicted and—where engineered—governed and replayed.

## 2. Verrell’s Law

Verrell’s Law is a **separate proposed falsifiable retained-state selection research framework**.

Current non-trivial empirical claim:

> Under controlled conditions, changes in selection log-odds are predicted by a preregistered retained-state compatibility score through a stable, directional coupling within a declared test regime.

The canonical mathematical specification and empirical-identification clarification remain the primary research authorities:

- [Mathematical Foundations and Falsification Protocol v1.0](VERRELLS_LAW_MATHEMATICAL_FOUNDATIONS_AND_FALSIFICATION_PROTOCOL_v1.0.md)

CAAI engineering behaviour is not treated as proof of a universal physical law.

## 4. Current Commercial Evaluation Pattern

The preferred first commercial purchase is a bounded decision problem rather than an immediate full middleware licence.

```text
one real or anonymised decision problem
+ a bounded set of permitted candidate actions
+ relevant retained history where required
```

Current commercial path:

1. Retained-State Decision Audit.
2. Bounded Core Gold evaluation.
3. Buyer-specific paid pilot.
4. Integration / larger licence discussion where justified.

Buyer-facing demonstration pattern:

```text
same present condition
+ different retained history
→ measurable selection difference
→ disable retained-state influence
→ comparison/reference result
→ replay
→ inspectable evidence
```

This is an engineering/evaluation method. It does not itself validate Verrell’s Law as a universal scientific law.
