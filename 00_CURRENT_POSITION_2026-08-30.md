## Research position retained from this snapshot

**Retained-State Selection** remains the technical process/category language:

> **Retained-State Selection is the controlled study or process by which information preserved from prior states is permitted to influence selection among presently available candidate outcomes.**

The general ideas of retained state, memory, path dependence, hysteresis, stateful computation, candidate scoring and policy engines are not claimed as original.

Verrell’s Law remains a proposed falsifiable framework asking whether retained history contributes measurable, candidate-relative influence over later selection and whether that influence can be isolated, compared, predicted and challenged through intervention / ablation.

A null result is evidence about the tested regime. Lack of detection is not evidence that an undetected mechanism exists.
