# What We Know vs What We Are Investigating

## What We Know

The following are supported by existing engineering, scientific, or computational evidence:

- Memory influences future behaviour.
- Retained information can alter future decisions.
- History-dependent systems exist.
- Learning systems are path dependent.
- Bias can be represented as directional weighting.
- Behaviour can be stabilised through governance and control layers.
- AI systems can drift.
- Continuity mechanisms can improve consistency.

## What We Are Investigating

The following remain research questions:

- Whether retained information can act as a general selection-bias term across multiple classes of systems.
- Whether observer and measurement effects can be usefully unified under information-update frameworks.
- Whether memory-like weighting mechanisms exist outside currently understood biological and computational systems.
- Whether broader physical analogues of memory-weighted selection exist.
- The precise physical mechanisms, if any, behind such effects.

## Important Boundary

This repository distinguishes between:

1. Established observations.
2. Engineering implementations.
3. Testable hypotheses.
4. Open research questions.

Not all statements carry the same evidential status.

The purpose of this distinction is to improve clarity, falsifiability, and scientific transparency.
