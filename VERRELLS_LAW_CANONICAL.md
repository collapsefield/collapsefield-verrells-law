**Author / originator:** Marcos Verrell Moss Ross (M.R.)

## Current claim

Verrell’s Law proposes that retained information from prior states can influence later selection in a measurable, directional and path-dependent way.

The current non-trivial empirical question is not simply whether history can matter. It is whether a **predeclared retained-state measure** predicts structured change in later selection under matched present conditions and whether that relationship survives held-out testing, intervention and falsification.

Compactly:

> **same present condition + different retained history → test for measurable selection divergence**

---

## Current mathematical spine

Within a declared test regime, the retained-state selection formulation uses a preregistered retained-state compatibility difference and tests its relation to candidate-relative selection change.

A first-order public form is:

```math
\Delta L = \lambda\Delta R
```

with confirmatory analysis allowing an intercept and residual term:

```math
\Delta L_k = \alpha + \lambda\Delta R_k + \varepsilon_k
```

The scoring rule, scale, sign convention, baseline and confirmatory analysis must be fixed appropriately before testing.

---

## What the framework does not claim

The current framework does **not** require or establish:

- a universal electromagnetic memory field;
- external storage of personal memory;
- consciousness as a universal selection mechanism;
- consciousness-caused quantum collapse;
- modification of general relativity;
- a new fundamental force;
- faster-than-light communication;
- software behaviour as proof of a law of nature;
- completed experimental confirmation.

Possible physical or biological mechanisms remain separate research hypotheses unless independently measured.

---

## Evidence rule

A result supports the framework in a tested regime only when the relevant retained-state variable is independently identified or appropriately preregistered, the effect survives controls, predicts held-out observations and responds correctly to intervention / ablation.

A null result under adequate conditions is informative. Measurement limitations may constrain an experiment, but lack of detection is not evidence that an undetected mechanism exists.

---

## Physics-facing work

The current physics-facing extension uses established open-system and information-thermodynamic machinery as the baseline.

Established non-Markovianity, hysteresis, Landauer-consistent heat or ordinary memory effects are **not** counted as Verrell-specific evidence by themselves.

The stronger question is whether a predeclared history-weighted variable identifies an additional, reproducible contribution beyond the best conventional explanation.
