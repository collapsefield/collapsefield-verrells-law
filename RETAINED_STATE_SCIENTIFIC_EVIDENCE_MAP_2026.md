> **Central distinction:** Established history-dependent effects show that retained state is scientifically real in particular systems. They do **not** establish a new general law, a universal memory medium, or the additional predictive contribution proposed by Verrell’s Law.

## 1. Why this map exists

Verrell’s Law is a proposed **falsifiable retained-state selection framework**. Its research question is whether history-dependent retained information, declared independently of later outcomes, predicts a **directional, measurable and intervention-sensitive** shift in selection under appropriately matched present conditions.

This note separates:

1. **External observations and models:** what the cited studies or reviews actually report;
2. **Relevant conceptual connection:** why that work matters when designing a retained-state test;
3. **Unresolved discriminating question:** what additional evidence, beyond accepted explanations, would be needed to support a Verrell’s Law claim.

The point is to strengthen experimental controls and acknowledge prior art, **not** to rebrand existing neuroscience, hysteresis or non-Markovian physics as new discoveries.

## 4. What would count as *additional* support?

The fact that a system has memory, a history-sensitive response, or an empirically successful conventional model is **baseline science**. To make an independent retained-state-selection claim credible:

1. **Specify the selection problem:** candidate outcomes, matched present system conditions, inputs and governing constraints; explain any unavoidable mismatch or latent state.
2. **Operationalise retained history:** declare what differs, how it is measured, and what physical or informational carrier is being manipulated, without assuming a universal field.
3. **Freeze the independent weighting rule:** preregister its sign, normalisation, candidate compatibility function and relevant coefficient or estimation procedure *before* examining confirmatory selections.
4. **Compare strong nulls:** include established memory allocation, latent-state, ordinary learning, hysteresis, non-Markovian, environment and measurement-error models as applicable.
5. **Test directional held-out selection changes:** require predicted log-odds or otherwise prespecified selection shifts under matched present inputs, not merely post-hoc stories about individual outcomes.
6. **Intervene or ablate:** modify, remove, reset or randomise retained state where ethically and experimentally feasible; ask whether the anticipated selection change follows.
7. **Seek robustness and replication:** check sensitivity, confounders, different histories, apparatus and independent groups; report null outcomes without rescue interpretations.

For the general framework the canonical log-odds and retained-state score definitions are in the linked mathematical and identification documents; **this map introduces no new equations or replacement criteria**. In software, confirming a selector obeys a weighting equation it was programmed to use is **engineering conformance**, not independent confirmation of a physical law.

## 5. Research boundaries

- **Biology:** Synaptic plasticity, neuronal engrams, glial interactions, embodied signals and environmental cue loops remain legitimate mechanisms. Research into distributed or non-neuronal participation does not invalidate neuroscience.
- **Physics:** Non-Markovian quantum processes and ordinary classical hysteresis are established frameworks of enquiry. The [September 2026 non-Markovian literature note](2026-09-14_RECENT_NON_MARKOVIAN_RESEARCH_ALIGNMENT.md) already covers process tensors, causal breaks and relevant thermodynamics in detail; avoid duplicating it here.
- **Fields and consciousness:** Field-mediated, resonant, quantum-level and consciousness-related proposals may remain open hypotheses where they can be operationalised and measured; they are **not** the required foundation or verified conclusion of the current retained-state framework.
- **Collapse:** In general public usage, selection resolution among candidates under present input, retained history and constraints; no default assertion of quantum wave-function collapse.
- **CAAI:** Collapse Aware AI is a separate engineering programme for governed retained-state behavioural selection. Demonstrating engineered history-sensitive software does not establish Verrell’s Law as physics.

## 6. Maintenance rule

When a new scientific result appears, record **publication and version date; study type; system/substrate; actual measurement; intervention/control; conventional explanations; relevance; limitations; potential falsifier**. Mark whether it changes experimental design, the prior-art baseline or merely provides contextual analogy.

**Update threshold:** Add only substantive, traceable work. Avoid changing the canonical law, mathematical protocol, website or CAAI commercial evidence claims because of a topical paper or review. If a result warrants a scientific amendment, document the exact reason and propose it separately for review.

**Current verdict:** These publications motivate careful retained-state modelling and better experiments. **They neither confirm nor refute Verrell’s Law in an independently tested regime.**
