## Current position

Verrell's Law is now framed as a **falsifiable retained-state selection research framework**.

The core research question is:

> When present conditions are held equivalent, can different retained histories produce a measurable, directional and intervention-sensitive difference in what is selected next?

The current framework does not require:

- a universal electromagnetic memory field;
- external storage of personal memory;
- consciousness as the selection mechanism;
- quantum collapse as the explanation of ordinary AI behaviour;
- modification of general relativity;
- software behaviour as proof of a natural law.

Earlier discussion of wider field-memory or consciousness mechanisms is historical/speculative unless independently measured.

---

## AI continuity boundary

The durable engineering observation behind the earlier note remains useful:

- storing history is not the same as governing its influence;
- context retrieval is not the same as candidate selection;
- retained history can be modelled as influence over presently available choices;
- authority and permissions should remain externally bounded;
- selection behaviour should be inspectable and testable.

Collapse Aware AI™ is a separate engineering programme exploring governed retained-state selection in software. Its engineering behaviour does not prove Verrell's Law as physics.

---

## Current mathematical route

The present canonical retained-state specification uses candidate-relative selection change and a preregistered retained-state compatibility difference, with the first-order relationship:

```math
\Delta L = \lambda\Delta R
```

The score definition, sign, scale, baseline and confirmatory analysis must be frozen appropriately before testing. Independent empirical evidence is kept distinct from engineering conformance testing of a selector deliberately designed to use retained-state weighting.
