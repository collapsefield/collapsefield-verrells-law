## Scientific Boundary

Verrell’s Law does not currently claim:

- completed experimental proof;
- completed peer review;
- established new physics;
- proof that consciousness collapses the wavefunction;
- proof of non-local personal memory;
- proof that current AI systems are conscious;
- proof that Collapse Aware AI validates the wider physical framework.

The current claim is deliberately narrower and is subject to preregistered falsification.

The correct negative verdict is:

> rejected or not supported in the tested regime.

It is not:

> disproven everywhere.

## Archive Principle

The archive is kept transparent by design.

Older documents are not removed simply because the framework has matured. Instead, current-status notes identify which materials are authoritative, supporting, historical or speculative.

This protects the authorship trail while keeping public interpretation disciplined and technically bounded.
