## Do Not Use As Evidence

Do not use the following as proof:

- AI agreement;
- Claude/Gemini/ChatGPT tone shifts;
- Reddit reactions;
- repeated numbers;
- personal coincidence;
- screenshots of LLM validation;
- post-hoc pattern matching;
- uncontrolled dashboards;
- unregistered tests.

These can appear as personal notes or development history, but not as validation of the framework.

---

## Current Evidence Standard

Evidence should use:

- matched present input;
- defined retained-history difference;
- pre-defined baseline;
- pre-registered confound list;
- estimator discipline;
- bias-on / bias-off or memory-active / memory-neutral comparison;
- reproducible divergence or clean null result;
- null wording: refuted in the tested regime.
