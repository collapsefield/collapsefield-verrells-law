## Correct Public Summary

In the quantum measurement-problem context, it proposes a **candidate memory-biased probability rule** in which standard Born-rule probabilities are reweighted by a bounded retained-information bias term.

## Safe One-Paragraph Summary

Verrell’s Law proposes that retained information from prior states may act as a measurable bias on future selection outcomes. In its quantum measurement-problem note, the framework introduces a candidate memory-biased modification to the Born probability rule, where outcome probabilities are conditionally reweighted by a retained-information bias term. The model recovers standard Born statistics when memory bias is absent and is explicitly framed as a falsifiable, bounded, and unproven research proposal requiring controlled experimental validation.

## Key Equation — Candidate Probability Rule

The public candidate rule is:

```text
                    |c_i|² · exp(β · B_i)
P_V(s_i) = ─────────────────────────────────
           Σ_j |c_j|² · exp(β · B_j)
```

Where:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `|c_i|²` | Standard Born-rule probability for outcome `s_i` |
| `B_i` | Retained-information / observer-state / history bias score for outcome `s_i` |
| `β` | Coupling sensitivity to the bias term |
| `P_V(s_i)` | Verrell-biased candidate probability for outcome `s_i` |

If `β = 0`, or if all `B_i = 0`, the rule reduces to the standard Born rule.

This equation is an ansatz, not a completed derivation.

In software analogues such as Collapse Aware AI, the observer role is played by a governed selection system with stored weighted memory.

## Bias Clarification

Bias does not automatically mean error.

Bias means directional weighting.

A bias term can improve continuity, recognition, prediction, and stability when aligned with the target system.

A bias term can also produce distortion, false confirmation, overfitting, hallucination, or prejudice when misaligned.

The framework therefore treats bias as a measurable selection influence, not as automatically good or bad.

Preferred wording:

> Bias is only an error when the weighting is misaligned with the target reality.
