## Status

---

## Field Activity

Verrell's Law does not depend on memory being stored in a non-local electromagnetic field.

The primary mechanism is retained state.

However, the framework does not rule out the possibility that biological, electromagnetic, quantum, environmental, or other field activity may participate in retained-state behaviour in some systems.

The disciplined position is:

> Fields may participate; retained state is primary.

If a field contribution to retained-state influence exists, it must enter the framework as a measurable coupling term with defined units, defined instrumentation, defined noise controls, and predictive value beyond ordinary biological, environmental, or computational retained-state variables.

No such general field-coupling measurement is currently claimed here.

---

## Physical Extension Boundary

Verrell's Law is not currently presented as established physics.

It does not currently modify general relativity, quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, neuroscience, or standard probability theory.

Whether structured retained information has any physical stress-energy contribution is an open, currently unformulated question.

No tensor extension is asserted in the current public framework.

## Consciousness Boundary

Verrell's Law does not claim to prove consciousness.

A cautious working interpretation is:

> Consciousness may be studied as an active process by which memory and information are organised into usable frames for interpretation and action.

That remains a research framing, not proof of a completed theory of consciousness.
