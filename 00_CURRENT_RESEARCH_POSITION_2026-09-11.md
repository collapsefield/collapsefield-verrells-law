## 1. Retained-State Selection

**Retained-State Selection** is the broad technical process / category language:

> Information preserved from prior states may be permitted to influence selection among presently available candidate outcomes.

The general ideas of memory, state, hysteresis, path dependence and stateful computation are established. They are not claimed as original.

The research question is narrower: whether a declared retained-state difference predicts a structured, directional, reproducible and intervention-sensitive difference in later selection under matched present conditions.

---

## 2. General mathematical formulation

The current retained-state selection specification uses a preregistered candidate-relative retained-state quantity and tests whether it predicts a change in selection log-odds:

```math
\Delta L = \lambda\Delta R
```

Independent evidence requires the rule producing `ΔR` to be frozen independently of the confirmatory outcome.

A deliberately engineered selector using its own internal score can provide engineering conformance evidence, but that is not automatically independent confirmation of the broader research hypothesis.

## 3. September 2026 physics-facing formulation

A new technical edition develops a separate physical formulation:

[**A Framework for Non-Markovian Information Paths**](VERRELLS_LAW_NON_MARKOVIAN_INFORMATION_PATHS_TECHNICAL_EDITION_2026-09.md)

It asks whether, after conventional system, environment, control and instrument memory are accounted for, a predeclared measure of physical transition weight identifies an additional reproducible contribution to the effective memory governing later dynamics.

This is a **proposed theoretical model**, not established physics and not experimentally confirmed.

Ordinary non-Markovianity, hysteresis, information erasure and quantum sensing are baselines and tools. They are not counted as evidence for the proposed additional term unless a preregistered discriminating prediction survives the required controls.

---

## 4. Evidence discipline

The physics-facing evidence ladder is:

1. Formal identifiability
2. Controlled history divergence
3. Intervention and ablation
4. Metrological and thermal convergence
5. Independent replication

A negative result in a well-defined regime is informative.

Future measurement sensitivity may matter to experimental design, but “detectors are not good enough yet” is not evidence that an undetected mechanism exists and must not be used to escape a null result.

---

## 5. Mechanism boundary

The current research position does not require:

- a universal electromagnetic carrier;
- non-local personal memory;
- consciousness-caused quantum collapse;
- information as a free-standing physical substance;
- modification of general relativity;
- a single mechanism shared by engineered, cognitive, biological and physical systems.

Different domains may instantiate retained-state structure through different mechanisms.

---

## 6. Engineering boundary

Engineered retained-state selection behaviour can be evaluated as software behaviour.

It does not prove a universal biological or physical law.

Research claims and software claims should therefore be evaluated independently, even where they share terminology or motivating structure.
