# Verrell’s Law

**Author / originator:** Marcos Verrell Moss Ross (M.R.)  
**Status:** Proposed falsifiable research framework. Not established physics.

Verrell’s Law is a retained-state selection framework concerned with a simple question:

> **When present conditions are held equivalent, can different retained histories produce a measurable, directional difference in what is selected next?**

The framework does not require a universal electromagnetic memory field, observer-controlled collapse, non-local personal memory, consciousness physics, or any supernatural mechanism.

Its present research programme separates three layers that must not be conflated:

1. **Retained-state selection** — the general controlled study of whether preserved prior information influences later selection.
2. **Formal / empirical testing** — preregistered scoring, matched present conditions, held-out prediction, intervention and falsification.
3. **Physics-facing extensions** — separate hypotheses about physically instantiated retained information, open-system memory and measurable history-dependent dynamics.
