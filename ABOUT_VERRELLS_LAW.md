# Verrell’s Law

**Author / originator:** Marcos Verrell Moss Ross (M.R.)  
**Status:** Working, falsifiable retained-state selection research framework  
**Updated:** 4 November 2026 — current scope clarification; foundational proposition retained

## Central proposition

Past interactions can leave persistent state changes. Retained information can subsequently bias weighting and selection, producing path-dependent outcomes under comparable present conditions.

> **When present conditions are matched, can different retained histories produce a measurable, directional and intervention-sensitive difference in what is selected next?**

Interaction → state change → retained information → weighting/bias → selection → path-dependent outcome.

**Retained state is primary; no particular field, biological or quantum mechanism is required.** Storage and influence may be instantiated through measurable biological, computational, material or environmental state. Electromagnetic and other fields can participate where specifically measured; universal EM memory, non-local personal memory and consciousness-induced quantum collapse are neither required nor established.

## Mathematics and evidential progress

The canonical exponential-tilt model specifies a retained-state score added to present-state selection. Its exact pairwise identity is:

```math
\Delta\log\mathrm{odds}(a,b)=\lambda(R_a-R_b)
```

This is an algebraic identity under the declared finite-score, common-positive-support assumptions. Whether independently specified scores predict observed selection beyond established history-sensitive models remains to be tested.

The November 2026 mathematical reviews examined alternative memory aggregators and retrieval mechanisms and produced synthetic model-comparison results and an **unrun discovery-pilot design**. They improved the precision and testability of the research, but did not provide independent human or physical evidence. See [Research Status and Testing — November 2026](VERRELLS_LAW_RESEARCH_STATUS_AND_TESTING_2026-11.md).

## Research layers

1. **Retained-state selection** — define the history-dependent variable and its candidate-relative influence.
2. **Formal and empirical tests** — independently freeze scores, match present conditions, test interventions and held-out predictions against prior-art models.
3. **Physics-facing extensions** — separately investigate physically instantiated memory, open-system dynamics, EM-field interactions and candidate quantum hypotheses, with their own consistency tests.
