## 1. What Verrell’s Law Is

Verrell’s Law is a testable retained-state selection hypothesis.

It proposes that the evolution of a system may depend not only on present state and current input, but also on structured information retained from prior states.

The non-trivial claim is not simply that memory can change behaviour. It is that, under controlled conditions, a preregistered retained-state compatibility score should predict a stable, directional change in later selection log-odds within a declared test regime.

In compact form:

> Retained history changes weighting.  
> Changed weighting changes selection.  
> The predicted relationship must survive controlled measurement.

Read it together with:

The clarification states what `R` / `ΔR` mean in an empirical test and distinguishes:

- an independent empirical test using a preregistered analysis score;
- an engineering conformance test using a selector’s own internal score;
- proxy-based inference where an independently measured proxy stands in for canonical `R`.

Its first-order claim remains:

```math
\Delta L=\lambda\Delta R,
\qquad
\lambda>0
```

where:

- `ΔL` is the change in candidate-relative selection log-odds;
- `ΔR` is the preregistered, normalized retained-state compatibility difference;
- `λ` is a regime-specific coupling interpreted only under the declared scoring convention.

For independent evidence, the rule that produces `ΔR` must be frozen independently of the confirmatory selection outcome. Reusing the same internal score that mechanically drives a selector is useful engineering evidence, but is not by itself independent confirmation of the wider hypothesis.

## 3. What Verrell’s Law Does Not Claim

Verrell’s Law does **not** claim that it has already been experimentally proven.

It does **not** claim that consciousness or sentience has been proven in any system.

It does **not** replace established neuroscience or physics.

It does **not** require quantum collapse, a universal electromagnetic memory field, non-local personal memory, or a supernatural mechanism.

It does **not** treat Collapse Aware AI as proof that the wider hypothesis is true in nature.

Physical, biological, field-based and consciousness interpretations remain separate research layers requiring separate evidence.

## 4. What Makes the Claim Falsifiable

Generic context effects predict only that outputs may vary when history varies.

Verrell’s Law predicts a narrower structure:

- the compatibility score is defined before testing;
- its scale and sign convention are fixed;
- retained-state-on and retained-state-off conditions are controlled;
- the coupling is expected to be positive and stable within the declared regime;
- residual errors should not remain correlated with the retained-state score;
- the fitted relationship should predict held-out trials.

The confirmatory model is:

```math
\Delta L_k=\alpha+\lambda\Delta R_k+\varepsilon_k
```

A valid regime should support `α ≈ 0`, `λ > 0`, stability under repetition, and out-of-sample prediction.

The hypothesis is rejected or not supported in a tested regime when those conditions fail or when the preregistered falsification conditions are met.

## 7. Current Experimental Programme

A separate exploratory programme is investigating whether controlled software timing / drive-rate conditions add reproducible information about retained-state coupling or persistence.

This frequency/timing work is **non-canonical, unvalidated, and not evidence for a physical resonance mechanism**.

The current experimental hierarchy is:

```text
v0.2 — base exploratory frequency mathematics
v0.3 — retained-state conditioning + software start-phase controls
v0.4 — per-trial restore + identification + probe-reactivity controls
v0.5 — empirical-route clarification + power + mechanism/preregistration boundary
```

The next evidence-bearing step is not another mathematical extension. It is runtime characterization followed by a frozen preregistration.

Before a first confirmatory run, the programme requires:

- exact per-trial retained-state restore or a defensible read-only route;
- measurement of whether probes/selection mutate retained state;
- loop/update/sample cadence and timer-resolution characterization;
- a declared minimum effect worth detecting;
- power/trial-budget calculation;
- fixed stopping, exclusion and robustness rules;
- fully nested model/frequency search with untouched held-out evaluation.

For the first software retention experiment, the current exploratory choice is a simple condition-dependent, constant-within-run decay model (`B1`). More flexible time-varying/Floquet-style models remain deferred unless simpler models first produce reproducible evidence.

No additional physical apparatus is implied by this Track-S software programme. Physical-substrate / field-drive experiments are a separate Track-P programme with separate evidence and apparatus requirements.

## 9. Public Summary

> Verrell’s Law is a falsifiable retained-state selection hypothesis. It predicts that, within a declared and controlled regime, candidate-relative retained-state compatibility produces a positive, quantitatively structured change in selection log-odds relative to a present-state baseline. The hypothesis is rejected for a tested regime when that coupling is zero, negative under the declared sign convention, unstable, non-predictive, or attributable to uncontrolled variables. Independent empirical testing is kept distinct from engineering conformance testing of systems deliberately built to apply retained-state weighting.
