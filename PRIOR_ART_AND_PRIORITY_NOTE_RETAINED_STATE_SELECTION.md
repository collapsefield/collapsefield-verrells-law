# Retained-State Selection — Prior-Art and Priority Note

**Author / originator:** Marcos Verrell Moss Ross (M.R.)  
**Status:** Public provenance note  
**Updated:** 14 September 2026

The public record documents the development of the retained-state selection framing, terminology, mathematical hypotheses and test programme associated with Verrell's Law.

This does **not** claim invention or ownership of broad pre-existing ideas such as memory, stateful computation, path dependence, hysteresis, Bayesian updating, reinforcement learning, softmax/logits, policy engines or candidate scoring.

The narrower research position is the explicit candidate-relative retained-state variable together with an identification and falsification route under matched present conditions.

Timestamped GitHub commits and public deposits establish provenance for the particular expression and chronology of this work, not monopoly over generic adjacent concepts.
