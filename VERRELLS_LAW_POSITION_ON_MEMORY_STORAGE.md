# Verrell’s Law — Position on Biological Memory Storage

**Author / originator:** Marcos Verrell Moss Ross (M.R.)  
**Status:** Public research clarification  
**Updated:** 14 September 2026

---

## 1. Established biological memory is accepted

Verrell’s Law does **not** reject established neuroscience.

Biological memory is supported by extensive evidence involving mechanisms such as:

- synaptic plasticity;
- distributed neural ensembles / engrams;
- cellular and molecular state changes;
- hippocampal, cortical and amygdala involvement;
- network-level reconstruction;
- cue-dependent recall and reconsolidation.

The current framework does not require personal memory to be stored outside the brain or body.

---

## 2. The retained-state question

The narrower research question is:

> **When present conditions are held sufficiently matched, can a declared difference in retained history predict a measurable, directional and intervention-sensitive difference in what is selected next?**

In biological systems, the retained-state variable should first be sought in established measurable mechanisms.

A biological application therefore requires its own operational definitions, controls and evidence. Analogy alone is not sufficient.

---

## 3. Electromagnetic and field activity

Electromagnetic activity is an ordinary part of neural and biological function. That fact does not establish a separate electromagnetic memory mechanism.

The current position is:

> **Fields may participate where independently measurable; retained state is primary.**

A field-based extension would need to specify:

- a candidate source;
- a coupling path;
- a measurable observable;
- expected spatial / temporal / spectral behaviour;
- detector sensitivity and noise model;
- conventional biological and instrumental alternatives;
- an intervention or ablation capable of changing the predicted effect.

No universal electromagnetic memory carrier, non-local personal memory store or consciousness-mediated field mechanism is claimed as established here.

---

## 4. Physical experiments

Earlier development material explored optics, phononics, acoustic traces and other possible physical analogues.

Those proposals remain part of the historical research trail, not current evidence.

The present physics-facing route is defined more strictly in:

[Verrell’s Law — A Framework for Non-Markovian Information Paths](VERRELLS_LAW_NON_MARKOVIAN_INFORMATION_PATHS_TECHNICAL_EDITION_2026-09.md)

That route treats known non-Markovian dynamics, hysteresis, information thermodynamics and instrument memory as **baselines to beat** rather than as evidence to count twice.

A distinct history-weighted contribution would need to improve held-out prediction, follow a predeclared weighting relation and change appropriately under intervention.

---

## 5. Measurement limits

Insufficient detector sensitivity can be a legitimate experimental limitation.

It is **not** evidence that an undetected mechanism exists.

If no effect is detected under an adequately powered and well-controlled protocol, the correct conclusion is bounded to the tested regime and sensitivity.

---

## 6. Relationship to Collapse Aware AI™

Collapse Aware AI™ is a separate engineering programme for governed retained-state selection.

Software intentionally designed to preserve and use retained state can demonstrate engineering behaviour, replay and inspectability. That does not independently establish an equivalent mechanism in biological memory or physical systems.

---

## 7. Current short form

> **Verrell’s Law accepts established biological memory science. It asks a separate retained-state selection question: under matched present conditions, does declared retained history add measurable predictive influence over what happens next? Any stronger biological or physical mechanism requires its own independent evidence.**
