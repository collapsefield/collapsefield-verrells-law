## 8. What Counts as Relevant Overlap

Not every idea about memory, consciousness, electromagnetic fields, AI behaviour, or quantum measurement overlaps with Verrell’s Law.

General discussion of memory, EM fields, AI agents, or consciousness is not automatically derivative.

Relevant overlap becomes stronger when later material combines several of the following elements:

- memory as a biasing force on future state selection
- observer-conditioned collapse or probability bias
- retained information treated as an informational pressure
- Ψ / tensor-style informational stress framing
- Einstein-like field equations with memory or informational source terms
- memory-weighted collapse language
- AI middleware used as an applied testbed for collapse-bias theory
- memory changing future behaviour rather than merely being retrieved
- governed behavioural selection from candidate states
- anchor-based behavioural continuity
- governor-controlled drift reduction
- public terms or phrasing closely matching Verrell’s Law or Collapse Aware AI documents

A later work may be independent if it develops a clearly different structure, terminology, mechanism, and timestamp trail.

But renamed or lightly paraphrased versions of the above should be treated as potentially derivative and compared against the public record.
