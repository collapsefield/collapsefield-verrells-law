## 4. Current mathematical and empirical boundary

The current retained-state programme uses standard mathematical and statistical machinery.

A principal first-order relationship is expressed through candidate-relative selection change and a preregistered retained-state compatibility difference:

```math
\Delta L = \lambda\Delta R
```

with confirmatory analysis allowing:

```math
\Delta L_k = \alpha + \lambda\Delta R_k + \varepsilon_k
```

The originality claim is **not** invention of logits, softmax, probability theory or memory itself.

The research position concerns the explicit retained-state variable, the identification boundary and the falsification route.

For independent empirical evidence, the rule producing the retained-state measure must be frozen independently of the confirmatory selection outcome or otherwise specified so circular confirmation is excluded.
