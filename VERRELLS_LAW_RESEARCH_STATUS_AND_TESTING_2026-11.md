# Verrell's Law — Research Status and Testing Note
**Date:** 2 November 2026  
**Status:** Public research-status clarification; not experimental confirmation  
**Originator:** M.R.

## The proposition remains unchanged
Prior interactions may leave persistent state changes; retained information may bias subsequent weighting and selection, producing path-dependent outcomes when present inputs are otherwise matched. This is a working, falsifiable retained-state selection framework, **not an established physical law**. Physical fields and quantum extensions remain separate, unconfirmed hypotheses.

## What the recent private reviews established
Mathematical audits conducted 1 November 2026 rechecked the exponential-tilt selection family:

```math
p_{\lambda,i}=\frac{p_{0,i}e^{\lambda R_i}}{\sum_j p_{0,j}e^{\lambda R_j}}
```

For a fixed candidate set, common positive support and finite scores, its pairwise consequence is exactly:

```math
\Delta\log\mathrm{odds}(a,b)=\lambda(R_a-R_b).
```

This is an **algebraic identity within the model**, not evidence that nature follows it. When scores are unconstrained, they can reproduce any target distribution with the same positive support. A scientific prediction requires independent score construction, frozen before confirmatory outcomes, plus suitable conventional comparators.

The reviews compared provisional active-mean, weighted-mean, sum, regularised-pool, gain, sampling, capacity and threshold mechanisms. Neutral memories, weight gradients, competing memories and retrieval rules can give distinguishable **model predictions**. Some proposed forms overlap established averaging, ACT-R-style blending, episodic sampling and divisive-normalisation approaches. Novelty or superiority has **not** been established.

The analyses used **synthetic simulations**, not new human or physical observations; numerical model recovery is not independent validation. The provisional discovery protocol includes cued and uncued histories, matched baselines, strength variation, competing memories, retrieval checks and reminder-update controls. It has **not** been run, preregistered or confirmed. A controlled language-model pilot could test analysis machinery or the behaviour of that particular model, but would not establish biological memory or physical claims.

## Open questions and next evidential standard
Research must distinguish **stored**, **cued**, **retrieved**, **activated** and **influential** information. In the existing mathematical definitions, `q_j` is **reliability**, and composite `w_j=s_j d_j q_j` combines salience, persistence and reliability. Five memories were an illustrative example, not a fixed retrieval capacity. No aggregation formula or retrieval policy from the audits has been adopted as new canonical law.

The next useful result would be a frozen, independently specified, falsifiable prediction that survives held-out evaluation against relevant prior art. A distinctive cross-domain rule is a research goal, **not a finding**. Physical-law status requires a separate, substantially higher evidential standard.

## Historical physics caution
A historical Born-rule reweighting ansatz is mathematically normalised but **not thereby physically consistent**. A particular nonlinear outcome-tilt construction examined in the audits can violate no-signalling at *any* nonzero bias; making the bias small limits detectability but does **not** prove exact no-signalling. A candidate-independent subtractive time term cancels under normalisation and cannot represent decay by itself. Historical informational-tensor expressions also have unresolved compatibility conditions. These are scoped corrections to exploratory extensions, not revisions of the retained-state core proposition.
